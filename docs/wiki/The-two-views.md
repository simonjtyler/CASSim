# The views

The calculator apps have three views. Handheld and Tablet show the calculator itself; Computer gives a larger layout for a keyboard and mouse. All three work by touch.

## Handheld

Looks like the calculator: a screen with the on-screen keypad below it. Tap or click the keys, or type on a keyboard. On a phone this view fills the screen: the screen and the keys both reach across it.

- Drag the bar between the screen and the keypad to change their sizes. In Tablet, tap **<** or **>** beside the bar to put the keypad on the other side of the screen.
- The keypad letters can be ABC or QWERTY (see [[Settings]]).
- In full screen (CASSim ▸ **Fullscreen**) or the installed app, the calculator's screen grows taller to use the whole height, up to the top edge of the phone.
- In the installed app on a phone, the screen uses the camera cutout area by default. Choose CASSim ▸ **Avoid cutout** to keep the calculator within the safe area (a ✓ shows it is on), and choose it again to use that space. The choice lasts until the app is closed or reloaded.

## Tablet

The calculator with the keypad beside the screen instead of below it, for a tablet or a phone on its side. Everything else is as in Handheld, including the bar between the screen and the keypad.

## Computer

A larger layout for a laptop or desktop with a hardware keyboard, with a page list and the variables beside the screen. The keypad can be shown beside the screen when you want it.

Drag a line between two columns to resize them. Tap **<** in the middle of the line to move the column on its right one place left, or **>** to move the column on its left one place right. For a header on each column that you can drag to move it (a bar shows where it will go), turn on **Show column headers** in CASSim ▸ **Settings**. To move and resize the columns from the keyboard, turn on **Show keyboard-accessible column controls** there too: Tab then reaches the arrows and resize buttons (they are left out otherwise, so Tab and Enter stay the calculator's keys).

Click **Pages** or **Variables** to hide or show each section. On a Problem's first page, use its
button to collapse or expand the other pages. Both sections scroll when needed.

Variables with the same dotted prefix, such as `stat.` or `tvm.`, can be collapsed together;
they remain separate variables. Lists and matrices start on one line, and their **…** button
expands them into numbered items or rows. Click the button again to collapse them. Long values show
more items in batches with **Show more**.

On a tablet, tap the entry line (or a spreadsheet cell) and the device's own on-screen keyboard opens, so you can type there too.

## Switching

Choose the view you start in under **Doc, Settings & status, Handheld setup** (see [[Settings]]). CASSim remembers it. The first time it opens, a phone starts in Handheld (or Tablet, held on its side) and a wider screen in Computer. CASSim ▸ **Computer view**, **Tablet view** or **Handheld view** switches at any time (Alt+Shift+C, T or H). On a phone the view follows the way you hold it: Handheld upright, Tablet on its side. There is not enough room on a phone for Computer view and the on-screen keyboard, so CASSim ▸ **Computer view** is greyed there.
