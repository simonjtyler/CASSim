# 0042. CASSim is released under the MIT licence

Date: 2026-11-28. Status: accepted (Simon).

## Decision

CASSim is released under the MIT licence, with the copyright line "Copyright (c) 2026 Simon Tyler
and CASSim contributors". It was released under the Unlicense before. The year is the project's
first (its first commit was on 2026-11-01).

## What changed

- `LICENSE`, `pyproject.toml` (`license = "MIT"`), the About dialog (`about.LICENCE` and
  `about.COPYRIGHT`), the README, SRS (NFR-LEG-003), PLAN and AGENTS say MIT.
- The About dialog's third-party notices also name CodeMirror and Flutter and its packages, whose
  full list ships as `assets/NOTICES`.
- LazyMath stays Unlicense; NFR-LEG-004 is unchanged.
