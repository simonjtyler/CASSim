About CASSim fx

CASSim fx is a non-graphing, non-programmable scientific calculator simulator built for Australian secondary mathematics curricula (Years 7–10, VCE, HSC).

Clean-Room Implementation: Contains no proprietary ROM, firmware, or brand artwork. CASSim is an independent simulator released under the MIT licence.

Privacy: Zero server tracking or telemetry. Saved states and URL share links run 100% locally in your browser.
